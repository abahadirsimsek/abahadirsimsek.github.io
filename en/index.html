Profile

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Health Care Management, Faculty of Health Sciences, Gümüşhane University. My research focuses on managerial decision problems addressed through mathematical modelling, multi-criteria decision analysis, and data analytics.

I received my PhD from Hacettepe University, Department of Business Administration, in 2019. My teaching and thesis supervision span operations research and decision analysis across health management, disaster management, and business programmes.

Research

Four research lines connecting methodological expertise with healthcare and management applications.

A

Operations Research & Optimization

Mathematical-programming approaches to resource allocation, scheduling, rotation, and two-sided matching problems.

B

Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis

Structuring complex managerial preferences through benchmarking, prioritisation, classification, and sensitivity analysis.

C

Health Systems Analytics

Analysis of health infrastructure, workforce, pharmaceutical consumption, and organisational performance using spatial, clustering, and decision-analytic methods.

D

Data-Driven Management

Transforming quantitative models into managerial decision support for inventory, sustainability, human resources, and AI-enabled applications.
